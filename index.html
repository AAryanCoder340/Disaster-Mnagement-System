Safety Tips for Coastal Disasters

Do's - Follow These Guidelines

  • Stay informed through weather updates and official alerts.
  • Keep an emergency kit ready (water, food, flashlight, first-aid, radio, power bank).
  • Move to higher ground immediately if there is a tsunami warning.
  • Follow evacuation orders from authorities without delay.
  • Keep important documents in waterproof bags.
  • Help children, elderly, and disabled persons first during evacuation.
  • Stay away from coastal areas and beaches during high tide or cyclone warnings.

Don'ts - Avoid These Actions

  • Don't ignore official warnings and advisories.
  • Don't attempt to go near the sea to "watch" big waves or cyclones.
  • Don't use electrical appliances in flooded areas.
  • Don't spread rumors or panic without confirmation.
  • Don't try to drive through flooded roads.
  • Don't leave pets and livestock unsecured.
  • Don't return to coastal areas until authorities declare it safe.

Additional Safety Measures

Before Disaster
  • Know your evacuation routes
  • Practice family emergency drills
  • Store emergency supplies
  • Secure loose outdoor items
During Disaster
  • Stay indoors and away from windows
  • Listen to emergency broadcasts
  • Turn off utilities if instructed
  • Keep emergency contacts handy
After Disaster
  • Check for injuries and hazards
  • Document damage for insurance
  • Avoid damaged structures
  • Report emergencies immediately
